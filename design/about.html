How I work

Designer who ships
01

Build the tool first

On two game-engine teams, designers needed editors before they could make levels. I learned to design for the person using the tool every day.

02

Audit, don’t trust

AI can draft fast. I check the numbers, states and edge cases it gets wrong, and I write down what I fixed and why.

03

Design for here

PayNow, hawker queues, Singlish microcopy. Local details make a product feel like it belongs.

Off the clock

Side quests

When I’m not pushing pixels, you’ll find me with a pen in hand, out with my camera snapping whatever catches my eye, or at the piano working out a song by ear.

Side quest 01

Pens & colour pencils

I draw with dry media, mostly ballpoint, fineliners and colour pencils. Slow, scratchy and very forgiving of a shaky hand. Flowers keep sneaking in, including the same orchids I photograph.

Side quest 02

Things that move

For my A Level art coursework I experimented with animation: tracing a figure frame by frame until a few white lines start to run, kneel and wave. It was my first taste of designing for motion.

Side quest 03

Piano, self-taught

To unwind, I sit down at the piano. No lessons, just patience, YouTube and a lot of replaying the same bar. Please be kind!

Side quest 04

Camera roll

I take my camera everywhere and take pics of whatever makes me stop: orchids, staircases, the odd suspicious bottle in a gallery corner, and the moments that matter to the people I love. You be the judge haha.
