Night at the Museum

A movie-themed board game brief. Our team chose Night at the Museum and turned the museum after dark into a board, an event deck and items that bend the rules.

TypeTabletop board game
TeamGroup project
ToolsFigma (no AI)
OutputBoard, cards, 40 tokens, printables
View in Figma ↗Pages: Cards, Tokens, Board, DLC, A4 printables, Green Light

Key decisions

  1. Movement tokens fix the direction.Crossing the museum takes planning, not just dice.
  2. The turn flow lives on the board.Nobody digs through a rulebook mid-game.
  3. Events as Museum News front pages.Headlines from the film make each event instantly recognisable.

01 · The board

The museum, as a grid.

The board is the museum floor as a grid, with the film’s exhibits as colour-coded zones: African Mammals, Miniatures, the Egyptian Temple of Ahkmenrah, American History and the corridors between.

Characters can’t move freely. Movement tokens fix the direction (right only, up only, diagonal), so crossing the museum takes planning. A legend explains every token, colour and spawn point.

02 · A turn

Seven phases, printed on the board.

The turn flow is printed on the board, so nobody digs through a rulebook mid-game:

  1. Draw character movement tokens
  2. Placement
  3. Random movement
  4. Capture or befriend
  5. Guard phase (the player moves)
  6. Capture or befriend
  7. Event: read the objective, place characters, roll for movement, then complete it

03 · Events

Every event is a front page.

Eight events styled as issues of Museum News, with headlines from the film: Theodore returns, Columbus sets sail, Anubis awakens, a stampede, a robbery. Each card shows the event and its reward: an item or a new party member.

04 · Items

Items with abilities and a quote.

Found items, like the Toy Keys, Gum Gum and Guard’s Flashlight, give active or passive abilities, each with a line from the film. Starting and event items, like the Tablet of Ahkmenrah, tie back to the events.
